Features

What it does

🔐

Login

Signs in to WeBOC or PSW. A wrong password stops after one attempt, so there is no lockout risk.

📋

GD List

Import or Export, by date range or month. Multi-page, no duplicates.

🧾

GD Detail

All GD data — packages, EIF/EF, items with duties, and bond entries.

📄

GD Download

Prints all parts and merges them into one PDF per GD.

💰

Balance

Fetches warehouse and local balances, stored only after the table fully loads.

📊

Data Posting

Posts data into the Master Workbook for further reporting and analysis.

🛡️

Safe by Design

Workbook backup before every run, pause and resume, failed GDs retried next time.

🔑

Centralized Credential Management

Securely manage application passwords and portal credentials for every customer and client from a single dashboard.

Do you see my passwords?

No. You enter them inside the app on your PC. They are stored only in your own encrypted profile.

Windows says "unknown publisher".

The app is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

What does the demo include?

Full features on GD data from the last 10 days. A subscription unlocks full history.

Can I move to another PC?

Yes. Copy your data folder and the app. Nothing is tied to one PC.

How do I renew?

Paste the new licence key or replace the file. No new installation is needed.
